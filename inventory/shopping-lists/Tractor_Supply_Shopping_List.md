# 🛒 Tractor Supply Shopping List — Bird & Squirrel Station

> For the next TSC run. Pull this up on your phone or print it.
> Companion to [Bird_Food_Inventory.md](../Bird_Food_Inventory.md).

**Last updated:** 2026-05-16

---

## 🐦 Bird Seed (Restock)

- [ ] Black oil sunflower seed — 40 lb bag (the staple)
- [ ] Safflower seed — 5–10 lb bag (cardinal favorite, squirrels skip it)
- [ ] Nyjer / thistle seed — small bag (for the bottom-port tube feeder)
- [ ] Mixed songbird blend — 20 lb bag
- [ ] Hot-pepper suet cakes (if they carry them — Royal Wing or C&S brand)
- [ ] No-melt suet dough cakes (for summer heat)

## 🐿️ Squirrel Station Supplies

- [ ] Whole dried field corn — 25–50 lb bag (Royal Wing wildlife corn)
- [ ] Raw peanuts in shell — unsalted, the bigger bag is cheaper per lb
- [ ] Corn cob spike feeder (screw-on tree-mount kind) OR a platform feeder

## 🛡️ Squirrel Deterrents (for the screen-room feeder poles)

- [ ] **Pole-mount squirrel baffle** — wraparound torpedo or stovepipe style, fits 1" shepherd's hooks (Audubon or Woodlink brand)
- [ ] **Hanging dome baffle** — clear plastic dome that goes ABOVE hanging feeders to stop drop-from-above squirrels
- [ ] **Cayenne pepper** — large container from the spice aisle (or bulk garden/pest section)
- [ ] **Mineral oil or food-grade safflower oil** — for the spicy seed recipe
- [ ] **Slinky** (optional, $5 hack) — slip over the pole, it bounces them right off

## 🧽 Cleaning & Misc

- [ ] White vinegar (gallon) — for the monthly feeder scrub (9:1 water:vinegar)
- [ ] Bottle brush set — for cleaning tube feeders
- [ ] Heavy-duty rubber gloves
- [ ] Disposable face mask — for mixing spicy seed

---

## 💡 Quick Notes

- **Best aisles at TSC:** Wildlife/bird food is usually in the back near the livestock feed. Baffles and feeders are often in a separate garden/outdoor section.
- **Bulk wins:** the 40+ lb bags are way cheaper per pound than the small bags
- **Check the Royal Wing brand** — TSC's house wildlife brand, good price-to-quality ratio
- **Ask at the counter** about hot-pepper products — sometimes they keep them behind the counter or with seasonal stock
