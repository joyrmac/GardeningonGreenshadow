- **Waning Moon:** Clear, prune, release. Burn dried herbs at the fire pit. Pull spent plants.

## Bonus: The "Maple Shrine" Decision

In `planning/Garden_Overview.md`, the area near the maple is listed as "Maple Shrine — no plan yet." This is your **6th potential sacred space** and the best candidate for an **altar spiral**.

### Recommendation: Build a 4–6 ft stone altar spiral here

- **Materials:** Fieldstones, river rocks, OR clay rolled into snakes (per Ancestral doc)
- **Shape:** Inward spiral if you want introspection/drawing-in energy; outward for releasing/expansion
- **Maintenance:** Refresh with seasonal items — fresh herbs from Potion Patch, dried bundles from Smudge Bundle station, seeds from Wayward Meadow
- **Optional center:** Small mirror, crystal, or just a flat candle stone

### Why the Maple Shrine spot is right for an altar spiral

- Already a "destination" spot (you walk to it deliberately)
- Has a tree (the maple) as a natural canopy/witness
- Not already programmed for another use (unlike the Potion Patch which has its own geometry)
- Visible enough to tend, not so prominent that it feels performative

## 🌒 Daily / Weekly Practice (Low Effort)

You don't have to do big rituals constantly. Small practices integrated into garden care:

- **When you water the Potion Patch:** speak intention to the plants. Even just "thank you, grow strong."
- **When you harvest:** name what you're taking and leave a pinch back at the offering bowl
- **When you mow or weed:** acknowledge what you're removing (and why), especially for invasives
- **When you light any candle outdoors:** it's a micro-ritual. Pick an intention.
- **When you check the moon phase before garden work:** the awareness itself is the practice
- **When you walk past the ancestor altar at Swingnook:** pause for 10 seconds

This is "the garden as my spiritual practice" — not separate, not performative, just integrated.
