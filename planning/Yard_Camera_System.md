## 🔧 Setup Notes

### Before You Buy

- **Wifi check first** — walk to each zone with your phone and run a speed test
- If signal drops below 10 Mbps in outer zones, add a **wifi extender** (~$30 TP-Link) or mesh node
- Reolink also has **4G LTE camera options** if any zone has no wifi reach at all

### Mounting

- **Height:** 8–10 ft (high enough animals don't notice, low enough for detail)
- **Angle:** slightly down, not bird's-eye — captures faces and markings
- **Facing:** north or east when possible to avoid sun glare
- **Test before committing** — set the camera loose in place for a day, watch the feed, then mount

### App / Privacy

- **Reolink app** runs all cameras from phone or tablet
- **Privacy zones** — black out neighbor's yard or street in the frame
- **Animal detection** — set per-camera alerts (person, vehicle, animal, pet)
- **Activity zones** — only trigger on motion in specific frame areas

## 🐈 Tom Cat Tracking

The hunter cat is part of why this system exists. Use the cameras to:

- Set **"animal" or "pet" alerts** on the feeder cams — get pinged when he shows up
- Track **timing of visits** (dawn/dusk is peak — confirms the indoor-during-those-hours strategy)
- Identify **his approach routes** so you can place deterrents on those paths
- See if the **Birdsbesafe collar** is actually deterring kills (footage doesn't lie)

## 🌟 ADHD-Friendly Tips

- **Start with Phase 1 only** — get the hub + 2 cameras working before adding more
- **Don't buy all 6 at once** — that's the express route to a box of unopened cameras in the garage
- **Set up one camera at a time** — install, configure, enjoy for a few days, then move to the next
- **Solar everything** — battery swaps are the #1 reason home camera systems get abandoned
- **Disable non-essential alerts** — too many notifications = muted app = no value. Start with 1–2 cameras alerting, expand if useful.
