# Garden Calendar Aligned with Holidays
## Your Year of Growing, Harvesting, Celebrating

Your garden **is** your spiritual calendar. This document maps when you plant, harvest, and preserve—aligned with your family celebrations and spiritual work. The garden doesn't just support the holidays; the holidays celebrate the garden's actual rhythm.

---

## How to Use This Calendar

**For each month/season:**
- **Garden Tasks:** What's actually happening in the dirt
- **Herbs Ready:** What you're harvesting and why
- **Holiday Connection:** Which celebration(s) use this harvest
- **Family Activities:** What Chad, Birdie, Murphy do in the garden
- **Preservation:** How to store what you've grown
- **Apothecary Work:** Teas, rituals, blends you're making

# WINTER (December, January, February)

# PRESERVATION METHODS & TIMELINE

## DRYING (Best: Herbs)
**When:** June-September
**Method:** Hang-dry, dehydrator, or sun-dry
**Herbs:** Rosemary, lavender, sage, mugwort, St. John's Wort, chamomile
**Storage:** Airtight containers, cool dark place
**Lasts:** 6-12 months

## FREEZING (Best: Basil, Fresh Herbs)
**When:** All season as you harvest
**Method:** 
- Basil in oil (cube tray, freeze, store in freezer bag)
- Herb sprigs in water (ice cube trays, freeze, store in freezer)
**Storage:** Freezer bags, labeled, dated
**Lasts:** 6-12 months

## INFUSING OIL (Herb-Infused)
**When:** Summer (fresh herbs) or use dried
**Method:** Pack herbs in oil, let infuse 2-4 weeks, strain
**Herbs:** Basil, rosemary, any culinary herbs
**Storage:** Airtight glass, cool place
**Lasts:** 2-3 months (fresh herbs; longer with dried)

## MAKING TEA BLENDS
**When:** Year-round (once herbs are dried/ready)
**Method:** Mix dried herbs to your preference; store in airtight containers
**Storage:** Cool, dark, airtight containers
**Lasts:** 6-12 months

# Practical Herb Growing Notes

**For Your Climate/Heritage:**
(Assuming temperate zone — adjust for your actual location)
